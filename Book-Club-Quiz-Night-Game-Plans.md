# Book Club Quiz Night — Game Plans

Three real-time games, each with a Participant view, Admin view, and Large LED Screen view.

---

## 1. The Famous First Lines Challenge

*Simple real-time quiz plan | Fast answers earn more points*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Format** | 10 first-line questions |
| **Players** | Up to 150 at the same time |
| **Timer** | 15 seconds per question |
| **Winners** | Top 2 |
| **Scoring** | Correct + faster answer = more points |
| **Answer type** | 4-option multiple choice |

### How the Game Works
1. A famous opening line appears on the participant's phone with 4 book-title choices.
2. The participant has up to 15 seconds to choose the correct book.
3. A faster correct answer earns more points than a slower correct answer.
4. The game moves through 10 questions and adds all points together.
5. The two highest total scores win.

### Points for Every Question
| Correct Answer Time | Points |
|---|---|
| 0–5 seconds | 100 points |
| 5.01–10 seconds | 75 points |
| 10.01–15 seconds | 50 points |
| Wrong / no answer | 0 points |

**Tie:** Same points = the participant with the lower total response time wins.

### User Side
- One line, 4 answer choices, timer and question number are shown at a time.
- The chosen answer locks immediately and the next question appears.
- At the end, the participant sees their total points.

### Admin Side
- Start and close the quiz.
- See live answers, points, response times and ranking.
- Confirm the final two winners before the LED winner reveal.

### Large LED Screen
- Before start: game title, one-line instruction and countdown.
- During play: show the question number, timer and live Top 10 scoreboard.
- Do not show the correct answer while participants are still answering.
- After the final question: reveal the Top 2 winners.

> **Live scoreboard example:** 1. Tharushi — 850 pts | 2. Zayan — 825 pts | 3. Malith — 800 pts

### Real-Time Flow

All participants answer the same question at the same time. Each question stays open for 15 seconds. Faster correct answers earn more points. After 15 seconds, everyone moves to the next question together.

| Stage | Participant / User | Admin | Large LED Screen / Live Scoreboard |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Before Start | Opens the game and waits on the start screen. | Makes sure the game is ready and starts it when the MC is ready. | Shows the game title, short instructions and READY message. |
| 2. Question Starts | Sees one famous first line, 4 book choices and a 15-second timer. | Watches how many people are playing and answering. | Shows the question number, 15-second countdown and live Top 10 scoreboard. |
| 3. Participant Answers | Taps one answer. The answer locks. A faster correct answer earns more points. | Sees points and rankings update as answers come in. | The Top 10 scoreboard updates as players earn points. The correct answer is not shown while the question is open. |
| 4. Next Question | When 15 seconds ends, everyone moves to the next question. The same process repeats for all 10 questions. | Keeps the quiz running and monitors the rankings. | Changes to the next question and keeps showing the live Top 10. |
| 5. Game Ends | After Question 10, sees their final points. | Checks the final ranking. If scores are tied, the participant with the faster total response time ranks higher. | Stops the live scoreboard and prepares the final result. |
| 6. Winner Reveal | The top two participants are announced as winners. | Confirms the final Top 2 before the result is shown. | Displays 1st Place and 2nd Place with the winner names and final points. |

The LED screen shows a live Top 10 scoreboard. Rankings can change after each question as participants earn points.

## 2. The Genre Crown

*Fiction first, then Non-Fiction | Separate crowns for each genre*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Genres** | Fiction and Non-Fiction |
| **Order** | Fiction round first, followed by Non-Fiction |
| **Questions** | 6 Fiction + 6 Non-Fiction |
| **Timer** | 15 seconds maximum per question |
| **Winners** | 1 Ruler of Fiction + 1 Ruler of Non-Fiction |
| **Main scoring idea** | Correct answers are worth more points when answered faster |

### How the Game Works
1. The Fiction round is played first by all participants.
2. Each Fiction question appears on the participant's phone with 4 answer choices and a 15-second timer.
3. After Fiction is completed, the Non-Fiction round starts in the same way.
4. Points are given only for correct answers. Faster correct answers earn more points.
5. The highest Fiction score becomes the Ruler of Fiction. The highest Non-Fiction score becomes the Ruler of Non-Fiction.

### Points for Every Question
| Correct Answer Time | Points |
|---|---|
| 0–5 seconds | 100 points |
| 5.01–10 seconds | 75 points |
| 10.01–15 seconds | 50 points |
| Wrong answer / no answer | 0 points |

**Tie:** If two players have the same points, the person with the lower total response time in that genre wins.

### User Side
- The participant sees one question at a time, 4 answer choices, the 15-second timer and question progress.
- Once an answer is selected, it is locked and the next question appears.
- After Fiction, the participant sees their Fiction points. The Non-Fiction round then begins.
- At the end, they see both of their scores.

### Admin Side
- Start Fiction, close Fiction, then start Non-Fiction.
- See live participation, scores and rankings for each genre separately.
- See which answers are correct and how quickly each participant answered.
- Confirm the final Ruler of Fiction and Ruler of Non-Fiction before the LED winner reveal.

### Large LED Screen
- Show the current genre clearly: FICTION ROUND or NON-FICTION ROUND.
- Show the question number and the 15-second countdown, but do not show the correct answer while the question is active.
- Show a live Top 10 scoreboard with participant names and points. The board updates as answers come in.
- At the end of Fiction, reveal the Ruler of Fiction. At the end of Non-Fiction, reveal the Ruler of Non-Fiction.

| Final Result | Admin confirms the ranking and the LED reveals the Ruler of Non-Fiction. |

## 3. The Book Match Challenge

*Simple real-time game plan | 12 book-related matches*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Format** | 12 matching pairs |
| **Players** | Up to 150 at the same time |
| **Timer** | 75 seconds total |
| **Winners** | Top 2 |
| **Scoring** | Correct matches + speed bonus |
| **Goal** | Complete as many correct matches as possible, as fast as possible |

### How the Game Works
1. The participant sees two sets of cards on their phone.
2. They tap or drag one item to its correct match.
3. A correct match locks in place. An incorrect match returns so they can try again.
4. The game ends when all 12 pairs are matched or when 75 seconds runs out.
5. The player receives points for every correct match plus a speed bonus for finishing all 12 quickly.

### Points
| Scoring Part | Points |
|---|---|
| Each correct match | 50 points |
| Finish all 12 in 0–30 sec | +400 speed bonus |
| Finish all 12 in 31–45 sec | +300 speed bonus |
| Finish all 12 in 46–60 sec | +200 speed bonus |
| Finish all 12 in 61–75 sec | +100 speed bonus |
| Not all 12 completed | No completion bonus; keep points from correct matches |

**Tie:** If two players have the same total points, the player who reached that score first ranks higher.

### User Side
- The phone shows 12 items and 12 possible matches, a 75-second timer and progress such as 7/12 matched.
- Correct matches lock immediately, helping the participant see what remains.
- The final score is shown once the challenge ends.

### Admin Side
- Start and close the challenge.
- See who is playing, number of correct matches, completion time and total points.
- See the live Top 10 ranking and confirm the final Top 2.

### Large LED Screen
- Before start: Book Match Challenge title, simple instruction and countdown.
- During play: live Top 10 showing name, points and progress (for example 10/12).
- Do not show the matching answers on the LED while the game is active.
- At the end: reveal 1st and 2nd place.

> **Live scoreboard example:** 1. Ruwan — 900 pts — 12/12 | 2. Sana — 850 pts — 12/12 | 3. Amali — 700 pts — 11/12

### Real-Time Flow

| Stage | Participant / User | Admin | Large LED Screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ready** | The game page opens, but the matching board is still locked. The player sees the game title, the 75-second timer, and a short instruction: match each book with the correct author, character or idea. | Checks that participants have joined and keeps the game locked until the MC is ready. | Shows the game title, a simple instruction and "Get Ready". No answers are shown. |
| **Countdown** | A 3...2...1 countdown appears. The player cannot select any cards yet. | Starts the game for everyone at the same time. | Shows the same 3...2...1 countdown so the whole room starts together. |
| **Game starts** | The 12 book cards appear on one side of the phone. The 12 possible matches appear on the other side. These matches can be authors, characters or book ideas. The 75-second timer starts. | Can see how many people are active and each player's current score/progress. | Changes to the live scoreboard view. It can show the current Top 10 with name, points and progress such as 5/12. |
| **Making a match** | The player first taps a book card. That card becomes highlighted. They then tap the author, character or idea they think belongs with that book. | Sees the player's score update as correct matches are made. | The scoreboard updates as players gain points. It never shows which cards are being matched or the correct answers. |
| **Correct / wrong** | If the match is correct, the two cards lock together and cannot be used again. The player gets 50 points and the progress changes, for example, from 4/12 to 5/12. If the match is wrong, the two cards simply unlock and the player can try again while the timer keeps running. | Can see correct-match count, points and who is moving up the ranking. | Top 10 positions can move up and down in real time as players earn points. Only names, points and progress are shown. |
| **Keep matching** | The player repeats the same process until all 12 pairs are correct or time runs out. Finishing all 12 gives an extra speed bonus, so completing the board faster gives more points. | Monitors the live ranking and watches for players who have completed 12/12. | Continues showing the live Top 10. A player who finishes 12/12 may move higher once the speed bonus is added. |
| **Finish / time up** | If the player completes all 12, the game ends immediately for them and their final score is shown. If 75 seconds runs out first, they keep the points from the correct matches they completed, but do not receive the full-completion speed bonus. | Closes the round when time is over, checks the final ranking and confirms the Top 2. | Stops the live scoreboard and changes to a short "Final Results" screen while the admin confirms the winners. |
| **Winner reveal** | Players wait for the result announcement. | Confirms 1st and 2nd place. If two players have the same points, the person who reached that score first ranks higher. | Reveals 1st and 2nd place clearly on the main screen. |
